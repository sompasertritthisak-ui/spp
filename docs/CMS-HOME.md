# The home page — a guide for SPP staff

The landing page of the website is yours to run. You can change what it says,
choose the products it shows, reorder or hide its sections, add blocks of your
own and put an announcement at the top — without a developer.

**Where:** Command Center → **CMS** → the gold **Home page** card → *Edit the home page*.

**Who:** anyone whose role includes **Content** with *edit* (the super admin,
admins and content managers). A role with Content *view* can open the screen but
not change it.

## The three steps — always the same

1. **Edit.** Change anything on the screen. Nothing is saved yet; the bar at the
   top says *Unsaved changes*.
2. **Save home page.** Your work is stored. The public website has **not**
   changed yet.
3. **Publish site** (top right). The website is rebuilt; visitors see the change
   about 2–3 minutes later.

You can save as often as you like and publish once at the end. *Preview live
page* opens the landing page that visitors see **now**, in a new tab — use it
after publishing to check your change.

If you try to leave with unsaved changes, the Command Center asks first.
*Undo changes* returns to the last saved version.

## The screen

**Left — the page, top to bottom.** Click a row to edit it on the right.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **↑ ↓** | Moves a section up or down the page. The hero always stays first. |
| **Switch** | Shows or hides the section. Hiding deletes nothing — switch it back on any time. |
| **Visible** | Visitors will see it. |
| **Hidden** | Switched off. |
| **Empty** | Switched on, but there is nothing to show yet (for example Featured products with no product chosen). The reason is written at the top of the section's editor. |
| **Check** | A field in that section needs correcting before you can save. |

**Right — the editor.** Every text has two boxes side by side: **English** and
**ລາວ**.

- The grey text inside an empty box is what the website shows now. **Leave a box
  empty to keep it.**
- Write in the English box to change the English. Write in the ລາວ box to change
  the Lao. If the ລາວ box is empty, Lao readers see your English text.
- The counter (for example `18/140`) shows how much room is left.
- **Reset to default** empties both boxes and brings the original wording back.
- In a headline, put `*asterisks*` around a word to set it as the accent:
  `See it *before* it exists.`

## How to add a product to the landing page

1. Make sure the product exists and is **Published** in Command Center →
   **Products**. Its photo, minimum order and lead time come from there.
2. Open **CMS → Home page** and click **Featured products** in the list.
3. Under *Products to feature*, type part of the name, then click the product.
   Add up to 12.
4. Put them in order with **↑ ↓**. Remove one with **×**.
5. If the section's switch is off, switch it on. Move the whole section up or
   down the page with the **↑ ↓** in the list on the left.
6. **Save home page**, then **Publish site**.

What the card shows:

- the product **photo** when it has one; otherwise SPP's own drawing of it;
- category and fabric, **minimum order** and **lead time**;
- *View product*, and *Design it* when the product can be designed in SPP Studio;
- a **price only** when online pricing is switched on and the product has a
  public "from" price. Otherwise no price is shown.

A product marked *draft — not on the site* in the list can be chosen, but it is
skipped on the website until it is published.

## How to add a custom block

Custom blocks are for anything the fixed sections do not cover: a paragraph
about a new service, a photo with text, a gallery, a video, a banner with a
button, a product grid, a price list, a map.

1. In **CMS → Home page**, find the **Custom blocks** card and press **Open the
   page builder** (the first time it says *Create and open the page builder*).
2. Press **Add section** and choose a kind — Text, Image, Gallery, Video, Call
   to action, Product showcase, and so on.
3. Fill it in and press **Save section**. Add more; reorder them with **↑ ↓**.
   Each block is saved as you go.
4. Go back to **CMS → Home page**. In the list on the left, move **Custom
   blocks** to where the blocks should appear on the page, and check its switch
   is on.
5. **Save home page** if you moved it, then **Publish site**.

Two things to leave alone in the page builder: the page's **slug** must stay
`home`, and its **status** must stay **Published**. If either changes, the
blocks disappear from the landing page (nothing is deleted — change it back).

Images and videos for blocks are uploaded in **CMS → Media library** first.

## The announcement bar

1. Click **Announcement bar**, switch *Show the bar* on.
2. Write the message — one short sentence — in English and, if you can, in Lao.
3. Optional: choose a page for the message to link to.
4. Choose gold or navy. The preview shows the result.
5. **Save home page**, then **Publish site**.

To take it down, switch it off, save and publish. A visitor who closed the bar
does not see it again during that visit; when you write a new message, everyone
sees it again.

## Buttons and links

*Goes to* offers the pages of the website, the pages built in the CMS and every
published product. For anything else choose **Another website (https://…)** and
paste the full address — it must start with `https://`.

The second button of the hero and of the closing band is optional: choose
**— No button —** to remove it.

## Good to know

- **Write what is true.** No invented numbers, client names, awards or
  testimonials. SPP does not advertise discounts or savings.
- **British spelling** in English: colour, visualise, customise.
- **Lao is never translated by machine.** What you type in the ລາວ box is what
  Lao readers see.
- Hiding a section keeps its text and choices for later.
- If something looks wrong after publishing, open the section and press **Reset
  to default**, save and publish: the original returns.

## What still needs a developer

- The inner text of three sections: the five verbs in the position statement,
  the five steps in "How we work" and the three feature rows in SPP Studio.
- The fixed notes that protect customers ("A request is not a booking…",
  "Mockups are a close visual guide…", the "Sample project" note).
- The layout, colours and drawings of the sections.
- A new *kind* of section or block that does not exist yet.
